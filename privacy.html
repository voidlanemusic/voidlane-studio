Privacy Policy

Effective: September 13, 2026

Overview

VOIDLANE Studio is a private desktop application used by its owner to prepare and publish original music videos to authorized social media accounts.

Information processed

When an account is connected, the application may process account identifiers, basic profile information, authorization tokens, creator posting settings, selected video files, captions and publishing status. It processes only the information needed to provide the requested connection and publishing functions.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate the authorized creator, prepare a post, upload creator-selected content, apply available privacy and interaction settings, prevent accidental duplicate submissions and display publishing status.

Storage and sharing

Authorization credentials are stored locally in the operating system's protected credential storage. VOIDLANE Studio does not sell personal information and does not share it with advertisers or data brokers. Information is sent only to the connected platform as required to perform an action requested by the authorized creator.

Retention and deletion

Local connection data is kept only while the integration is in use. The creator may disconnect an account or revoke access through the relevant platform settings. Local credentials can then be removed from the application.

Security

Reasonable technical safeguards are used, including protected local credential storage and platform-provided authorization mechanisms. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children

The application is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect children's personal information.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the application or platform requirements change. Questions may be sent through the official VOIDLANE Instagram account.
